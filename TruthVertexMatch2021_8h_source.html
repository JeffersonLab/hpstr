1/*
2 * @file TruthVertexMatch2021.h
3 * @brief Truth-A' -> reconstructed-vertex matching study for 2021 displaced A'/SIMP signal MC.
4 *
5 * Step 1 (this version): replicate the PreselectAndCategorize2021 truth block that finds the
6 * true A' (or SIMP vector) and its e+e- decay, and write the truth decay vertex + kinematics
7 * to a flat tree. Step 2 (later): search the reconstructed UnconstrainedV0Vertices_KF for a
8 * vertex matching this truth vertex (matching strategy TBD).
9 *
10 * Reuses the lean EventBus/Cutflow helpers from PreselectAndCategorize.h (no preselection
11 * machinery), mirroring ClusterEfficiency2021.
12 *
13 * @author Matthew Gignac
14 * @date June 2026
15 */
